Common questions about Jupiter

What is Jupiter, Florida known for in real estate?

Jupiter is known for gated golf-and-boating communities north of Palm Beach Gardens, with direct Intracoastal and inlet access, and a lower density and price-per-foot than Palm Beach island.

What are Jupiter's top private clubs?

Admirals Cove and The Bears Club are the two most prominent gated country clubs in Jupiter, both offering golf, tennis, and marina access.

Do I need a separate membership to buy in a Jupiter club community?

Yes. Club membership is a separate application from the real estate purchase and is not guaranteed by the brokerage. Some clubs carry waitlists, so it's worth starting that process early.

Who is Jupiter best suited for?

Families drawn to strong local schools, and boaters who want direct ocean access via the Jupiter Inlet, without Palm Beach island's density or price level.
